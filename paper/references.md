- **I1.** No numbered "I1" entry exists in RESEARCH.md; §I is an unnumbered bullet list. Mapped to its first bullet (METR / Redwood Research investigation), as stated in paper.md line 195.
- **G4.** RESEARCH.md lists "Ju et al., 2024" (arXiv). Published journal version is 2026 (Science China Information Sciences); reference uses the published version.
- **Title differences (cosmetic only).** The registry title differs slightly from the RESEARCH.md title for A6 (Crossref adds a trailing "algorithm": "... artificial bee colony (ABC) algorithm"), B1 (Crossref: "Metaheuristics – the metaphor exposed"), B2 (italic markup around "bestial") and C5 (italic species name dropped in the registry record). RESEARCH.md also appends informal system names in parentheses that are not part of the official title: E2 "(SwarmBench)" (arXiv title: "Benchmarking LLMs' Swarm intelligence") and A13 "(MAP-Elites)" (arXiv title: "Illuminating search spaces by mapping elites"). No substantive title mismatch was found. References above use the registry titles.
- **Year conventions.** Journal articles use the print-issue year (B1: online 2013, print 2015; B2: online 2022, print 2023; C3: online 2011, print 2012). Conference papers posted earlier on arXiv use the conference year (e.g. H1: arXiv 2023, ICML 2024). Preprints with no confirmed venue use the arXiv submission year.
- **Conference years for arXiv-hosted papers.** For E1, F1, F2, F5, G2, G8, G10, H1 and H2 the venue name was confirmed by the Semantic Scholar venue field (and by DBLP keys for F1, F5, G2, G8, G10); the conference year is taken from RESEARCH.md, consistent with the arXiv submission date.
- **A1** appeared in *ACM SIGGRAPH Computer Graphics* 21(4) (the SIGGRAPH '87 proceedings issue), as Crossref records it.
